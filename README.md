## 🇨🇳 中文

上传合同问「能不能签」，它不逐条念条款，而是先确认你代表甲方还是乙方，再按付款、交付、验收、责任、解除、保密、知识产权、数据、SLA 等交易模块找风险，而不是按合同标题审。结论强制分三层：必改风险、可争取优化项、形式完善项，风险等级分高 / 中 / 低。每条意见都给可复制的替换、新增或删除文本，事实不足时给保守版和折中版两套谈判选项。还会专门复查主合同与附件的金额、期限、赔偿上限是否打架。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 第一动作确认审查立场：未说明时默认按义务更重 / 更弱势一方临时审查，并在开头明确标注
- 按交易模块审而非按标题审，覆盖付款结算、交付验收、责任赔偿、解除终止、保密、IP、数据隐私、SLA 等 13 类线索
- 三层输出：必改风险 / 可争取优化项 / 形式完善项，并给每条的 高·中·低 风险等级
- 每条意见含位置、问题、为什么影响我方、建议动作、可直接复制的建议文本
- 对 .docx 先跑预检查脚本抓空白项、金额、比例、期限、附件与模块命中线索；脚本不可用则按同一清单人工检查
- 不确定时给保守版 + 折中版两套文本；主合同与附件的金额、期限、赔偿上限专项复查

## 🇬🇧 English

Upload a contract and ask whether it is safe to sign. Instead of reciting clauses, it establishes which side you represent, then reviews by transaction module, payment, delivery, acceptance, liability, termination, confidentiality, IP, data and SLA, rather than by contract title. Findings come in three forced tiers, must-fix risks, worthwhile negotiation points and formal clean-ups, each rated high, medium or low. Every finding carries paste-ready replacement, added or deleted clause text, and thin facts get both a conservative and a compromise option. A dedicated pass cross-checks amounts, terms and liability caps between the main contract and its annexes.

### ✨ What it does

- Confirms your stance first, defaulting to the obligation-heavier party and labelling the assumption
- Reviews by transaction module rather than title, covering payment, delivery, acceptance, liability, termination, confidentiality, IP, data and SLA
- Forces three tiers: must-fix risks, worth-negotiating optimizations and formal clean-ups, each rated high, medium or low
- Every finding carries location, problem, why it hurts your side, suggested action and paste-ready clause text
- An optional precheck script over .docx surfaces blanks, amounts, ratios, dates, annexes and module hits
- Gives a conservative and a compromise clause when facts are thin, and re-checks main contract against annexes
